## Small experiments, kept

Little Batch is a notebook for recipes that change one variable at a time. Coffee ratios, a loaf that finally behaved, and the version worth making again all get a little space.

![Recipe notebook illustration](/assets/little-batch.svg)

## Make the next try easier

Each note captures the ingredients, a quick method, and what I would change next time. It is part archive, part excuse to keep experimenting.
