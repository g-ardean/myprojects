## A place for the in-between

Reed & Air keeps the daily practice from disappearing into a blur. It is a gentle log for repertoire, warmups, recordings, and the notes that help the next session begin well.

![Music practice illustration](/assets/reed-air.svg)

## The rhythm

The journal can hold a short warmup checklist, a link to a recording, or a note about where a passage finally settled. It celebrates consistency as much as performance.
