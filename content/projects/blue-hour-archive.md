## Keep the moment attached

Blue Hour Archive is a personal index for photographs and the small stories around them. Each entry keeps the place, weather, and a sentence or two close to the image.

![Dusk landscape concept](/assets/blue-hour.svg)

## Why a small archive?

The best parts of a day are often the details you did not plan to collect. A lightweight archive makes it easy to find them later without turning the habit into a second job.
